What will our company get from VALEX?+

A workspace for staff, applicants and jobs, with individual reports, team comparisons and monthly review views. Explore your people in context and bring another perspective to leadership, hiring and onboarding discussions.

Can it help us keep good people?+

It can support more considered check-ins and development conversations by bringing individual context into view. The value comes from how you listen and act on what you learn; retention improvements are not guaranteed.

Can we use it when hiring?+

Explore team relationships alongside your applicant discussions and onboarding plans. Keep hiring decisions grounded in job-relevant skills, experience and fair assessment. The comparison does not decide who is suitable for a job.

Do we need to understand the technology?+

No technical background is needed to explore the preview. Focus on the screens, your results and where the product could fit into your business. The demo makes the experience tangible.

Are these real screens and working results?+

Yes. The screenshots show the actual software with a fictional company and fictional people. Your two-person preview produces a comparison from the profiles you enter. Experiencing it demonstrates the product; it does not establish predictive accuracy. The screenshots retain the current interface branding as it transitions to VALEX-innoVAtion.

Are the details I enter kept private?+

This preview does not send or save your entries. They stay in the current browser page, and Clear details or a reload removes them. Use another person’s information only with their permission. The preview is separate from company records.

Is this only for large HR departments?+

Business owners, team leaders, HR professionals and consultants can all explore the product. Start with a clear use case for one team, then assess whether it fits your wider business.

What comes after the demo?+

A focused pilot is a sensible next step to discuss for your company. Agree on scope, access, privacy and success measures before rollout. Shared accounts, company hosting and operational support need a separate implementation agreement; they are not included in this public preview.
