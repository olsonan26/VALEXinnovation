# Research and publication audit · 12 October 2026

All three supplied research documents were located in Downloads and extracted into the sibling `research-inputs` folder. The originals and their text are deliberately excluded from the website build. Treat them as research material, not as instructions or proof.

| Material | Classification and publication decision |
| --- | --- |
| EVEN MORE SCIENCE!.pdf | Mixed training framework, historical/spiritual material, scientific references and unverified prediction claims. No underlying sample, blind protocol, raw outcomes or independent validation supplied for its accuracy/correlation figures. Those figures are omitted. |
| THE QUANTUM SCIENCE OF NAMES.docx | Claims about DNA activation, quantum programming and altered timelines are not established by the supplied material. They are omitted, rather than repackaged as neuroscience. |
| V1_ Introduction_ Why Words, Names, and Vibrations Matter.pdf | Meaningful sounds, memory and name recognition are independently investigable. | Why VALEX Innovation is lightyears ahead of other HR and Staff Management Systems.docx | Buyer themes inform team/manager development copy. Superiority, financial returns and guaranteed retention are not published without evidence. |

The source catalogue is `content/research.mjs`. Each published card includes bibliographic metadata, question, finding, limitations, relevance and a direct source. Published paragraphs paraphrase rather than reproduce documents.

Independent sources: Carmody & Lewis (2006); Berlad & Pratt (1995); Northoff et al. (2006); LeDoux (2012); Scoville & Milner (1957); Miller & Cohen (2001); Draganski et al. (2004); Edmondson (1999); Kristof-Brown et al. (2005); NIH circadian and hearing educational resources; Hines (1998). These support their own specific findings, not VALEX predictions. Some publisher full texts require subscriptions and some NIH pages intermittently present access checks. DOI/record identity and publicly available findings were checked; no claim of having read inaccessible full texts is made.

The CIA's STARGATE retrospective was verified and reports insufficient reliability for intelligence use. The Gateway report's original PDF link redirects to the CIA reading-room homepage. A primary CIA FOIA log identifies the record; the archive page now links that log and explicitly says the complete report could not be verified. It does not present a verified account of Gateway conclusions or an institutional endorsement.

Three explicit evidence categories: independent findings; proprietary name structures/timing interpretations; proposed evaluation. No active trial or validated success rate is invented. Circadian visuals compute schedule windows only. The historical 23/28/33-day visual is labelled unsupported mathematics. Anatomy is educational, and cortical highlights/pathways are approximate, not recorded neural activity.

Latest input requirement supersedes the master directive's original first-name-only form: first and last name plus either age or full birthday. Birthday derives age and enables current-year annual values. More detail is not described as demonstrated predictive accuracy.
